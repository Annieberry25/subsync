## 4. Alternate hooks

Swap the first line, keep the rest of the caption. Rotate these across posts and
let the numbers pick the winner.

1. `Your bank account has a leak. It's called auto-renew.`
2. `The money isn't missing. It's on repeat.`
3. `Check your subscriptions before you check your balance.`
4. `Free trials don't end. They just start charging.`
5. `You're not bad with money. You're just subscribed to too much.`

---

## 5. Posting notes

- **Pin a comment:** `What's the subscription you forgot about? Be honest.`
  It is the cheapest engagement signal available to you.
- **Reply to every comment in the first hour.** Answer questions; do not argue.
- **Links:** organic posts — link in bio only (TikTok and Instagram suppress
  caption links). Paid ads — use the platform's button, not a caption URL.
- **Rotate hooks.** Do not reuse one caption everywhere; run the five variants
  and keep the one that holds attention past 3 seconds.
- **First 1.5 seconds decide everything.** The caption stops the scroll; the
  video has to keep it. No caption guarantees a viral post.

---

## 6. Accuracy guardrails

- **The "2.6 subscriptions" figure needs a source before it is repeated in copy.**
  If there is no source, write `more than two` instead.
- **Keep the verb honest:** SubHalt *warns before the charge*; the user cancels.
  Never say "stops the charge", "cancels for you", or "blocks the payment".
- Do not name subscription providers in post copy or hashtags.

## 7. Music reminder

- Use CapCut's **Commercial use** tab, or YouTube Audio Library.
- Do **not** use trending sounds for ads; they are not licensed for commercial use.
- One 20–25s bed, cut to the frame hits, around −14 LUFS for social. No voiceover.
